Privacy policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026.

Mac Mini Encrypted Backup is used only by its owner to create and restore encrypted recovery archives in the owner's Google Drive.

Google Drive access is limited to files created or otherwise explicitly used with this application through the drive.file scope. OAuth credentials are kept in private local configuration. A recovery key is stored locally and separately by the owner in a password manager; neither the key nor OAuth credentials is included in the uploaded recovery archive.

Backup content is compressed and encrypted on the Mac before upload. Google Drive receives encrypted archives and minimal integrity metadata. Backup content is not used for advertising, sold, or sent to AI models for training or inference by this tool.

Actual backups are normally at least seven days apart. At most two verified cloud recovery versions are retained after successful rotation; superseded application backup files are moved to Google Drive Trash. Trashed files remain subject to Google Drive's retention and the owner's Trash management.

This information website has no login, forms, analytics scripts, cookies set by its own code, or third-party assets. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process standard hosting request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

The owner may stop the local schedule and revoke this application's access in their Google Account. Existing backup files are managed separately in Google Drive; revoking access alone does not delete them. Questions and deletion requests should be directed to the app owner through an existing private contact channel. Do not send secrets or backup files through public channels.
